1. Data use guidelines

Geographic data provided in the attached package is for the single, non-commercial use of the purchaser, to conduct analysis, perform research, and create maps.

*** Under no circumstances may the base data--in part or in whole--be transferred to other individuals, either for free or for purchase. The data cannot be used for commercial purposes; this requires a different license. ***

Base data that have been transformed by the purchaser may not be transferred to other individuals unless with the express permission of Yonah Freemark and Steven Vance.

Any use of this data--for example, in the course of conducting research and producing maps--must be credited. A recommended reference is:

Yonah Freemark, Steven Vance, and OpenStreetMap contributors (2026). The Transport Politic: Transit Explorer. Available at https://www.thetransportpolitic.com/transitexplorer

There is a field associated with each feature, called "reference," which indicates the original data source; if using data (primarily historic streetcar and interurban data) that includes attribution beyond OpenStreetMap contributors, Yonah Freemark, and Steven Vance, please provide credit the relevant sources.

3. Before use, note...

Line files are sometimes separated into portions based on information available (most segments are separated based on opening year). As such, it is *not* appropriate to use a table directly exported from the geographic data to provide summary statistics.

For example, the Blue Line in City X may be separated into two segments, one completed in 2014, the other in 2028. It would be inappropriate to suggest that City X has two Blue Lines, however.

In addition, data provided in the database are based on what is available. As such, City X may have information about the dates that transit lines opened than City Y. As such, the data presented here should *not* be presented as all-encompassing of information about transit.

Finally, data on historic (closed) streetcars are incomplete and not as reliable as the rest of the data in the package. Please use this information carefully.

Use the data cautiously!
